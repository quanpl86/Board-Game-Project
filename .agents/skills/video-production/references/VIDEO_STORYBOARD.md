## Production
1. Confirm game feature exists or label concept/simulation clearly.
2. Script with Vietnamese voice segment timing, shot list, approved media/source.
3. Editable source (Remotion or NLE); pin dependencies, media manifest and fonts rights.
4. Record/generate authorized visuals, voice, audio; optional sound-off version for autoplay web.
5. Generate SRT/VTT with accurate word timings, safe zones and relevant audio-description fallback.
6. Render to requested format, inspect file metadata, duration, first-middle-last frames, captions and audio levels.
7. QA and content/rights signoff before public CDN release.

## Fail-safe
If no render tool or output verification available: deliver storyboard/source instructions, **do not claim MP4 rendered**. No unauthorized voice clone, copyrighted music or child identity footage.
