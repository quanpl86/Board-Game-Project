# Đặc tả Leaderboards & Hall of Fame
**Version:** 0.1 | **Status:** Proposed / not implemented | **Owner:** Competition + Community + Safety

## 1. Tách xếp hạng và vinh danh
- **Leaderboard:** bảng số liệu **biến động**, kết quả được xác minh, có metric, bộ luật, phạm vi, khoảng thời gian, phép chuẩn hóa và quy tắc đồng hạng.
- **Hall of Fame:** danh sách **vinh danh được tuyển chọn/xác nhận**, lưu thành tích gắn với kỳ sự kiện, đội, hồ sơ được phép hiển thị và ảnh/kỷ vật; không biến mất khi mùa giải chuyển sang kỳ mới.
- **Achievement:** huy hiệu/cột mốc chứng minh hoàn thành thử thách; có thể được hiển thị trong profile, album, tủ đồ (cosmetic frame).
- **Academic rubric:** đánh giá cá nhân theo học liệu, mặc định riêng tư, không tự xuất lên bảng xếp hạng công khai.

## 2. Các chiều xếp hạng
| Family | Metrics examples | Scope | Visibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Game specific | Elo/Glicko rating (where appropriate), score, wins, streak, accuracy | game, mode, season | public nickname if eligible |
| Tournament | verified standings, points, tie-break | event, bracket, division | participants, spectators under policy |
| Team/Club | team tournament score, cooperative challenge score | club, organization, season | team name safe public |
| Classroom | group score, class collective performance | class/school | class/group display only, not private grades |
| STEM competence | rubric attainment and self improvement | individual student | learner, teacher, authorized guardian only |
| Challenge speed | time, correct tests, resource efficiency | problem version, task seed | policy dependent |
| Seasonal | event tokens, campaign completion | season, public group | privacy policy |
| Community honors | approved project contributions | community / curated | voluntary nomination/review |

`metric_direction` = HIGHER_IS_BETTER/LOWER_IS_BETTER; `normalization_version`, `score_policy_version` and tie-break config must be explicit.

## 3. Ranking flow
1. Game or Coding judge emits authoritative verified outcome; `match_results` / `judge_runs` pinned to version.
2. Score Policy generates signed/auditable `score_records` in immutable ledger, stores source event and idempotency key.
3. Projection worker updates `leaderboard_entries` and `leaderboard_snapshots` with `calculated_at`.
4. Tournament director freezes final standings. Appeals create correction record and fresh projection, not overwrite source evidence.
5. Award system grants eligible trophies/frames/title after confirmed result; Hall of Fame nomination references verified result.
6. Hall of Fame uses approval/review/publication workflow and can be unpublished for privacy with audit.

## 4. Hall of Fame content
Categories: nhà vô địch theo game, giải STEM xuất sắc, đội hợp tác tiêu biểu, nhà thiết kế sáng tạo, thành tích giải thuật/coding, thành tích cộng đồng, giải lễ hội/tháng. Entry fields: `honor_id`, title, category, time_period, approved display handle/team, organization display opt-in, evidence link/replay, achievement definition, badge/trophy asset, hero/banner asset, description, localization, guardian/safety publication approval where required, publication revision, withdrawal status.
An optional `honor_nomination` workflow tracks proposer, source, reason, verifier, reviewer, publication status and expiration; no unreviewed awards generated by AI.

## 5. UI in KingDragonHub style
- Header dark ink, orange section eyebrow, strong typographic title, light surface.
- Tabs: Game, Thành tích, Đội, Trường, Sự kiện, Theo mùa.
- Filters: time (daily/weekly/monthly/all-time), game, level/age division, region/organization and verified status. Show data updated timestamp, tie-break explanation and pagination.
- Podium top three (trophy visuals) plus bordered ranking table/cards; profile name pseudonym by policy.
- Hall of Fame separate editorial gallery with event image, achievement, award date and validated evidence; carousel or grid of commemorative cards.
- Share cards for eligible adults/teams and opt-in public participant profiles; school admin controls minors' exposure.
- Empty, pending verification, frozen, corrected, loading and restricted states require explicit UX.

## 6. Fairness & safety
- Never let client submit final score as truth; per-game anti-cheat and replay review.
- Different difficulty levels cannot be mixed without approved normalization.
- Avoid leaderboard pressure for under-age users; classrooms prioritize team effort/individual improvement privately.
- No paid purchases affecting leaderboard metrics or game win conditions.
- Honors are earned/provenance-based, not buyable; cosmetics may decorate the display but cannot replace achievement evidence.

## 7. Access / acceptance
- `rankings.read` scoped by visibility; `rankings.policy.propose` for admin; `rankings.finalize` requires organizer/referee; `honors.nominate`, `honors.review`, `honors.publish` separated and logged.
- Tests: duplicate score event no double count; recompute after correction; frozen season remains traceable; wrong-tenant ranking denied; academic rubric does not leak; withdrawn honors disappear publicly but preserve audit.
