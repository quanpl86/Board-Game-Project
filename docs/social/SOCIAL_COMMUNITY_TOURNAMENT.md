# Social, Teams, Messaging, Leaderboards & Tournaments — Platform Specification
**Status:** Architecture draft · **Audience:** mixed-age public and school users.

## Groups
Support class learning groups, invited game teams, tournament teams, clubs and organizations as distinct entity types. Membership scopes and roles should not collapse into one generic game-room participant. Teachers can allocate groups randomly, manually or by controlled competence mix; suppress stigmatizing labels.

## Communication
Channels: class, assigned group, in-game room, event, tournament and moderated STEM community. Direct messaging is disabled by default for minors, especially between strangers. Quick Chat presets for younger pupils; older school users may use moderated text if approved. Public community publishing is reviewed for minors. Threads, reaction, pin, polls, read status, rate limits, file/link policy, report and mute.

## Moderation & privacy
ABAC authorization for channel membership; separate safeguarding roles, moderation queue, escalation and retention. Prevent unrestricted access by platform operations staff to children's private chats. Record moderation decisions with actor, reason and audit metadata; data requests processed under documented policy.

## Teamwork during gameplay
Eco City Lab team roles: Finance, Planner, Environment, Facilitator. Shared 3D world, group proposals and policy-driven majority/captain/unanimous approval. Chat and voting never bypass authoritative building rules.

## Leaderboard dimensions
Game, team, class, organization, event, tournament, seasonal, skill rubric, improvement and social achievements. The score ledger is immutable; projected standings can be rebuilt. Metrics are versioned per game and metric direction high/low; tie-breaks and age/grade partitions explicit. Never default to public individual education performance or low-performance lists.

## Competition lifecycle
Event program → registration/eligibility → tournament format & stages → fixtures → game room/match → referee verification → result finalization → score ledger → standings/rewards. Round robin, single/double elimination, Swiss, score/time attack, seasonal league and inter-game contests. Match settings and random seeds fixed per competition policy. Support walkovers, disputes, replay and corrections.

## Community
STEM topic spaces, moderated student showcases, read-only 3D city exhibits, replies based on learning feedback template. No unapproved personal contact sharing. Optional school/club-only channels.

## Acceptance
Cross-tenant access denied; unauthorized direct messaging blocked; teacher can mute/freeze classroom; competition results cannot be fabricated client-side; ranks rebuild after correction; reports route to safety staff; performance and privacy audited.
